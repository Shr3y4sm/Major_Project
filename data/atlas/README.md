# Educational atlas assets

`MNI152_T1_2mm_brain.nii.gz` is an MNI152 2 mm brain template obtained from
the MNITemplate distribution maintained by J. Fortin. `AAL_atlas.nii.gz` is
the Automated Anatomical Labeling (AAL) atlas distributed with atlasreader.

These files are used only to show an *educational*, atlas-normalized anatomy
context after an affine patient-to-template registration. They do not provide
patient-specific vessels, white-matter tracts, eloquent cortex, navigation
coordinates, or surgical margins. Those layers must come from registered,
same-patient clinical source data and receive clinician review.

Sources:

- https://github.com/Jfortin1/MNITemplate
- https://github.com/miykael/atlasreader
